Community Rules

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Airport notes are written by pilots for pilots. A moderator checks every note before other users see it.

  • Share what helps other crews at that aerodrome: taxi, stands, procedures, local practice.
  • Be accurate. Notes are information only — always follow official documents, NOTAMs and ATC.
  • No personal information about anyone, such as names, phone numbers or email addresses.
  • No offensive language, harassment, advertising or spam.
  • Nothing confidential from your company or anyone else.

Moderators may decline, hide, correct or remove notes, and may close an account or device that breaks these rules. You can report a note from its menu in the App.
